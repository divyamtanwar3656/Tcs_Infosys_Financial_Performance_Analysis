# Financial Performance Analysis — TCS vs Infosys (FY2019–FY2023)

A Python data analysis project comparing the financial performance of two of India's largest IT companies — TCS and Infosys — across 5 fiscal years using real balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow data.

# Questions Answered
Which company grew revenue faster over the 5-year period?

Which company is more profitable on a margin basis?

Which company generates better returns for shareholders (ROE)?

How did operating cash flow trend for each company?

Did COVID-19 (FY2021) visibly impact either company's financials?

# Key Findings
Infosys grew revenue faster — up ~77% from FY2019 to FY2023 vs TCS's ~54%.

TCS leads on every profitability metric — higher net margin (~20% avg vs ~18%), higher operating margin (~27% avg vs ~25%), and significantly higher ROE (~40% avg vs ~27%).

TCS's ROE is exceptionally high because it consistently returns cash to shareholders through dividends and buybacks, reducing equity on the balance sheet — the same profit over a smaller equity base produces a higher ROE.

COVID-19 (FY2021) had minimal impact on both companies — margins held steady, reflecting the surge in global demand for IT services and digital transformation during the pandemic.

FY2022 and FY2023 saw the sharpest acceleration in revenue for both companies, driven by post-COVID enterprise spending and currency tailwinds.

TCS's operating cash flow grew consistently from ₹28,593 Cr to ₹41,965 Cr. Infosys's CFO plateaued after FY2021, a trend worth monitoring.
